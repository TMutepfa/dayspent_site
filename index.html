DaySpent

Check in on your day, and find a short reset that fits it.

DaySpent is a daily check-in app for working adults 18 and over. Rate your day, name how you feel, and get a small self-care reset that fits the time you have. There's also an AI guide, career-based group chat and a weekly summary.

DaySpent is a self-care app, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. If you need help now, see our crisis lines by country, or call your local emergency number.
