Delete your DaySpent account

DaySpent, by Takudzwa Mutepfa · Last updated November 14, 2026

You can delete your DaySpent account and its data at any time. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open DaySpent and log in.
  2. On the Today screen, tap the circle with your initial at the top right to open Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away. If you'd like a copy first, tap Export my data in Profile before deleting.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email dayspentsupport@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my DaySpent account”, from the email address you signed up with. That's how we confirm the account is yours. We'll delete the account within 7 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address, password and profile: display name, career, age, gender and recharge style.
  • All your check-ins, notes, resets, ratings and saved resets.
  • Your Guide conversations.
  • Rooms you joined, every Lounge and private message you sent, and every photo and GIF you shared, including the image files.
  • Your private conversations, which are removed for the other person too, and your message requests, blocks and the reports you made.
  • Your AI permission choice.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports others made about you: these stay so moderators can finish reviewing them, but they're no longer linked to your account. The reported messages themselves are deleted with your account.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider's short-term technical logs, which may include your IP address, expire automatically within its standard log period.
  • AI requests: if you used AI features, OpenAI may keep Guide and suggestion requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes them.
  • Copies others already have: a backup or screenshot someone else made of a message you sent is outside our control.

Delete only some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove some of your data:

  • Lounge and private messages, photos and GIFs: press and hold your own message, then choose Delete for everyone.
  • Guide conversation: in the Guide, open the ••• menu and choose Clear this conversation.
  • Sending data to OpenAI: turn off AI features in Profile.

To ask us to delete anything else, email dayspentsupport@gmail.com.
