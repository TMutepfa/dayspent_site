Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

DaySpent is a daily check-in app for working adults. This policy explains what DaySpent collects, why, who it goes to, how long it's kept, and how you can see or delete it.

DaySpent is run by Takudzwa Mutepfa (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about your privacy, email dayspentsupport@gmail.com.

If you live in Washington State, or another place with a consumer health data law, also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

The short version

  • Your check-ins, journal notes and Guide conversations are private to you. Other members never see them.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Nothing goes to our AI provider, OpenAI, unless you allow AI features. You can turn them off at any time.
  • You can export your data or permanently delete your account in the app at any time.

How we use it

  • To suggest a short reset that fits your day and the time you have.
  • To run the AI guide, if you allow AI features.
  • To show your weekly summary.
  • To run the Lounge: group rooms for people in a similar career, private messages, and online status.
  • To keep people safe: to spot signs that someone may be in crisis and show support options, to check images before they are shared, and to review reports.
  • To run, secure and fix the service, and to contact you about your account.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

AI features and OpenAI

Some features use OpenAI, an AI company. DaySpent sends nothing to OpenAI unless you choose “Allow AI features”. We ask once after sign-up, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile. Without AI features, check-ins, suggestions from our reviewed activity library, your weekly summary and the Lounge still work.

FeatureWhat is sent to OpenAI
Reset suggestionsYour check-in note, feeling, productivity rating, time available and time of day, plus your age, career, gender and recharge style, and resets you've liked or disliked. OpenAI also searches a fixed list of trusted health websites for ideas
The GuideYour message, your recent Guide conversation (up to 20 messages), your display name and your latest check-in
Voice notesYour recording, to turn it into text
Photos and GIFsEach image you share, and its caption, for an automatic safety check before anyone else sees it

Your email address is never sent. OpenAI doesn't use this data to train its models. It may keep Guide and suggestion requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes them. It doesn't keep voice recordings sent for transcription or images sent for safety checks. If a check-in, Guide message or Lounge message suggests someone may be at risk, the app shows crisis support instead of a normal reply.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run DaySpent, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase hosts our database, logins, file storage and live chat.
  • OpenAI provides AI features, only if you allow them, as described above.

Both process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms, and may not use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of DaySpent, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

What other members can see

Other members of your career lounge can see your display name, your career, whether you're online, and the messages, photos and GIFs you choose to send. They never see your check-ins, journal notes, resets, Guide conversations, age, gender or email. Your display name doesn't have to be your real name.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Export my data.
  • Correct it: edit your profile in Profile at any time.
  • Turn AI features off: Profile → AI features.
  • Delete messages: press and hold your own message or image, then choose Delete for everyone.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete my account, or follow our account deletion instructions. This permanently deletes your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to know what we hold about you, to have it deleted, or to withdraw consent. To use any of them, email dayspentsupport@gmail.com from the address on your account. We'll reply within 45 days. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections. Database rules ensure that only you can read your check-ins, notes and Guide conversations, and that Lounge content is visible only to people in your career lounge. Images are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Adults only

DaySpent is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Not a medical service

DaySpent is a self-care app, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. If you're struggling, see our crisis protocol. In the United States, call or text 988 at any time, or call 911 in an emergency.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
